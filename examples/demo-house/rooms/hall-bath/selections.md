# Selections: Hall bath

Every product, finish and idea for this room: what's chosen, what's still open, what was
considered and passed on, and suggestions not yet decided. Updated whenever a link is
shared, an option is rendered, or something is picked.

Status: **idea** (suggested, nobody's looked yet) · **considering** (options on the table) ·
**chosen** · **ordered** · **installed** · **existing** (already there, staying).
Prices as seen on 2026-10-29.

## Open decisions

- Faucet: any 4" centerset in satin nickel, to match the vanity's knobs.
- Switches: there's no wall inside on the door's latch side, so the lighting review puts the
  3-gang box (two dimmers and a fan timer) in the hall at the latch side, 48" up. Confirm.
- Heat: no register or radiator is modeled. Find where the heat comes from today and add it.
- Tub valve access: the old access panel behind the sink goes away with the vanity. The Delta
  valve services from the front (the cartridge comes out through the escutcheon); keep a panel
  only if the shutoffs are behind the wall.

## Lighting, fan and switches

**Status:** chosen (2026-10-29, from a lighting designer's review: the `trades` agent)

The room had one light over the mirror and nothing else; the evening renders went dark past the
vanity. The review's plan: three wet-rated wafers, a real bar over the mirror, a quiet fan over
the tub, all 2700 K and CRI 90+, on dimmers.

- **Halo HLB4LSFS5 4" canless LED wafer, x3.** About $25 each ($90 for a 4-pack), Home Depot.
  Selectable 2700–5000 K (set to 2700) and lumens (set to about 600); wet-location listed.
  - Link: https://homedepot.com/p/332661944
  - Spec: https://www.cooperlighting.com/global/brands/halo/12015789/hlb-slim-low-glare-canless-led-downlights/hlb4lsfs5
  - Where: over the tub (15, -42), over the toilet (48, -30), at the entry (42, -74). About 44"
    apart, none over the mirror. The two near the tub are in the code's wet/damp zone.
- **Kichler 5337NIS 24" three-light vanity bar, brushed nickel, satin etched glass.** About $63.
  - Link: https://www.kichler.com/sku/5337NIS
  - Specs: 24" W x 8" H, 8" extension, 3 x A19 (use 800 lm, 2700 K, CRI 90+), damp-rated.
  - Where: centered over the mirror, bottom at 77" (3" above the mirror). The 31-1/2" sink bay
    is too tight for sconces beside a 24" mirror.
- **Panasonic WhisperCeiling DC FV-0511VQ1 exhaust fan.** About $150–180.
  - Link: https://jacksonsystems.com/product/panasonic-ventilation-fv-0511vq1/
  - Specs: 50/80/110 CFM selectable (set to 80 for duct losses; the room needs 50), under 0.3
    sone, 4" duct to the outside, rated over a tub on a GFCI circuit.
  - Where: over the tub at (15, -12), near the exterior north wall.
- **Lutron Diva DVCL-153P dimmers, x2** (cans, vanity bar), about $28 each, and a **Lutron
  Maestro MA-T51MN countdown timer** for the fan, about $40.
- **GFCI receptacle** moved to the south end of the backsplash, the far side from the tub (it
  was within 3 ft of the tub). One 20 A GFCI circuit for the room.

Materials: about $375–425. Before ordering: check what's above the ceiling and which way the
joists run (the cans may shift 3" to miss one).

**Considered:**
- Sconces either side of the mirror: no room (3-1/2" each side in the bay).
- A fan just outside the curtain at (40, -14): fine if the inspector doesn't want one over the tub.
